## Why MPL-2.0

MPL-2.0 is a file-level copyleft license designed for reusable software
libraries:

- Anyone may use the library, including in commercial or proprietary products.
- Source files containing Samsarix Agent Engine code, and modifications to those
  files, remain available under MPL-2.0 when distributed.
- A larger application may use different terms for its own separate files.
- License and copyright notices in covered source code cannot be stripped.
- Contributors provide the copyright and patent grants stated in the license.

MPL-2.0 does not require user-interface attribution and does not reserve a
commercial-use monopoly. The separate [NOTICE](NOTICE) file makes ownership and
contact information easy to preserve, while [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md) keeps
brand rights separate from the source-code license. [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff)
makes voluntary academic and project citation straightforward.

## Historical revisions

Changing the license for the current tree does not revoke permissions already
granted for copies of historical revisions under the license files distributed
with those revisions. The current source tree and the `0.1.0` release candidate
use MPL-2.0.

The removed `LICENSE.PROPRIETARY` remains available in Git history for provenance;
it does not apply to the current source tree.

## Contributions

Unless a contribution explicitly says otherwise before acceptance, it is
submitted under MPL-2.0, the same license as the project. Samsarix LLC does not
require a copyright assignment or contributor license agreement at this stage.
That keeps contribution terms simple, but it also means Samsarix LLC cannot
unilaterally relicense third-party contributions later.

If future dual licensing becomes a business requirement, obtain legal advice and
adopt a contributor agreement before accepting contributions that need to be
available under both licenses.
